UX Case Study

Mobile Banking App Redesign

Improving financial literacy and accessibility for the next generation of digital natives.

[Hero Image Placeholder - Replace with App Mockup]

Role

Lead UX Designer

Timeline

3 Months (2024)

Tools

Figma, Principle, Maze

The Challenge

Users were finding it difficult to track their monthly spending habits due to a cluttered interface and hidden features. The goal was to simplify the navigation and introduce a more intuitive way to visualize financial data without overwhelming the user.

The Solution

We designed a card-based dashboard that prioritizes daily spending limits and quick actions. By reducing the cognitive load and using progressive disclosure for complex data, we increased user engagement and satisfaction.

[Solution High-Fidelity Mockups]

Design Process

Our process followed the Double Diamond methodology. We started with user interviews to understand pain points, followed by rapid wireframing and iterative testing.

[Process / Wireframes Image]

Results

Post-launch analytics showed a significant improvement in key metrics.

+45% Daily Active Users
-20% Support Tickets
4.8 App Store Rating
