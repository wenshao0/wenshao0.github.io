Profile

Working across journalism, product, and information systems.

I am completing my master's at UC Berkeley, where I have been building an AI-powered data search and news discovery tool and exploring how information can be made more useful through product thinking, reporting, and clear structure.

Before Berkeley, I worked as a tech journalist covering AI and semiconductors, and I also collaborated on data visualization and analysis tools that turned fragmented information into clear, actionable insight.

Journalism trained me to investigate, verify, and explain complexity clearly. Product and data work strengthened my ability to design workflows, structure messy inputs, and help people make decisions. I bring those strengths into reporting roles, product roles, and other work that depends on making complex information useful.
